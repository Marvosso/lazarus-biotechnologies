## Overview

Lazarus Biotechnologies is a fictional biotechnology R&D and U.S.
federal technology contracting organization used as the basis for a
persistent enterprise cybersecurity learning environment.

The project is designed to simulate normal business operations,
enterprise technology, human error, security events, and evolving
cybersecurity posture.

The objective is to develop and demonstrate hands-on experience across
enterprise cybersecurity disciplines while documenting the complete
journey from initial architecture through ongoing security operations.

## Disclaimer

Lazarus Biotechnologies is entirely fictional.

All employees, organizations, government programs, research data,
controlled information, incidents, and business activity represented
by this project are synthetic and exist exclusively for cybersecurity
education and portfolio development.

No actual classified information, CUI, patient information, government
data, or customer data is used.
